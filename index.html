The Artist

Real life.
Real stories.

Big Solis is an independent Hip-Hop/Rap artist from Lodi, California, turning heartbreak, regret, ambition, loyalty and everyday life into music. His sound moves through rap, melodic textures and Latin influences while keeping honest storytelling at the center.

“Rolitas tristes desde el corazón.”

The songs feel like pages from a journal — music for late nights, long drives and anybody carrying something they need to get off their chest.
